# 01 — Vision

## Product Name

Shakti Sports AI

## Positioning

India's AI-powered talent discovery platform for athletics.

## Mission

To ensure that no athlete's future is limited by where they are born, who they know, or whether they can access professional coaching.

## Vision

To become the most trusted AI-powered athlete discovery network in India, starting with athletics and expanding into other sports after proving the model.

## Core Belief

Talent exists everywhere.

Opportunity does not.

Shakti Sports AI exists to close that gap.

## Problem

Millions of young athletes across India train with limited access to:

- professional coaching
- performance analysis
- scouting visibility
- structured feedback
- digital sports profiles
- verified performance records

Many talented athletes are never discovered because they live outside major cities, lack connections, or cannot afford elite training infrastructure.

## Solution

Shakti Sports AI enables athletes to upload performance videos, receive AI-assisted movement and performance analysis, and become discoverable by coaches, scouts, academies, and sports organizations.

## Initial Focus

The MVP will focus on athletics, especially sprint-based events, because athletics is measurable, widely accessible, and suitable for computer vision-based analysis.

Initial events:

- 100m sprint
- 200m sprint
- 400m sprint
- Long jump
- High jump

## Primary Users

### Athletes

Young athletes who want objective feedback and visibility.

### Coaches

Professionals who want to discover and evaluate talent efficiently.

### Scouts

Talent identifiers who need structured search, verified performance reports, and regional discovery tools.

### Academies

Training institutions that want to identify promising athletes.

### Admins

Platform operators who verify users, moderate uploads, and manage reports.

## What We Are Building

A focused talent discovery platform where an athlete can:

1. Register
2. Create a profile
3. Upload a performance video
4. Receive an AI-assisted performance report
5. Become visible to coaches and scouts
6. Receive opportunities

## What We Are Not Building in MVP

We are not building:

- a social media feed
- tournament management
- live scores
- team management
- merchandise
- nutrition plans
- fitness tracking
- chat-first networking
- multi-sport support from day one

These may come later, but they are not part of the MVP.

## North Star Question

Every product decision must answer:

Does this help discover hidden athletic talent?

If the answer is no, it probably does not belong in Version 1.

## Five-Year Vision

In five years, Shakti Sports AI should become a national-scale athlete discovery network where:

- athletes from villages and small towns can be evaluated fairly
- coaches and scouts can discover talent beyond traditional networks
- AI reports help standardize early-stage performance evaluation
- regional talent pipelines become more transparent
- India can identify more high-potential athletes earlier

## Brand Promise

Every athlete deserves a fair chance to be seen.
